Divergent thinking helps you open the possibility space. Convergent thinking helps you narrow it into a decision. Invergent practice is about learning to move deliberately between the two.

↗ DivergeGenerate alternatives, associations, perspectives, and unusual possibilities.
↘ ConvergeCompare evidence, constraints, trade-offs, and choose a defensible direction.
⇄ SwitchOpen first. Close second. Learn to change gears without getting stuck.
