Thinking practice lab

Train the way your mind moves.

Divergent thinking helps you open the possibility space. Convergent thinking helps you narrow it into a decision. Invergent practice is about learning to move deliberately between the two. Session scores are heuristic practice indicators, not definitive measures of thinking ability. They show how you performed in this exercise set, not a fixed “type” of thinker.

About the timer: It is a maximum, not a target. Finish early and continue immediately. Waiting for the countdown to reach zero does not make a response more divergent or convergent.
